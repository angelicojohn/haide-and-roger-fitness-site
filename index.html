Questions

Before you ask.

Still unsure? Message us, we’re happy to help.

Ask on Messenger
How much is the membership?

Regular monthly is ₱2,200 and a walk-in day pass is ₱300. Right now the BER Months Promo gives you 8+1 or 6+1 months at ₱1,500 (20 slots per package), and the 6-month lock-in is ₱7,500. See the full rates above.

Is there a registration fee?

Message us and we'll confirm the current fee for your plan before you pay anything.

Asa mo dapit? Where exactly are you?

2nd floor of the Talisay Tourism Multi-Purpose Building, La Revolucion, Poblacion, Talisay City, Cebu. Tap Directions below to open Google Maps.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Saturday 6:00 AM – 9:00 PM, Sunday 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM. Holiday or special hours are posted on our Facebook page.

How do I sign up?

Message us on Messenger, call 0927 795 1525, or just walk in during opening hours. When you sign up as a member, your first personalised session with one of the owners and a certified coach is free.

Do you have student, senior or PWD discounts?

Yes: 20% off for Talisaynon students, government and uniformed employees, senior citizens and PWDs. Bring a valid ID.

Can seniors train here?

Yes. We run guided personal training for seniors, with medical clearance from your doctor. Stronger at every age.

How do I apply for a job?

We're hiring a Personal Trainer and a Yoga Instructor. Send your CV through Messenger or come in for a walk-in interview.
